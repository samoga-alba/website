A little more about me

01 — Background

Driven by a multinational background and a passion for law, language, and technology, I am a student focusing on how data meets global communication. I founded SaMoGa Group, a digital project dedicated to records and data preservation.

My journey balances academic discipline —publishing research papers and high quality projects— with a strong creative side as a published poet and guitar player.

Projects

05 — Projects

Apart from SaMoGa, I have other projects, most notably the board game Traverse. This game is a school project in which players traverse the British Isles, learning the native historical languages of our homeland.

For this project, I obtained permission from Citroën, Hyundai and Koenigsegg to use scale miniatures of their cars as playing pieces.

I also collaborated with An Post, the Government of Guernsey (States of Guernsey) and Kent County Council to provide accurate representations of their respective regions and services, along with beautiful illustrations of the constituent islands.
